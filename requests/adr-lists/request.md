### R1 ADRs named in a list
A paragraph also names an ADR when it writes the number after "ADR" or "ADRs" with a space, in a list, or in a range, for example `ADR 0040`, `ADRs 0003, 0005 and 0040` or `ADRs 0003 to 0005`. The rule on a citation of a superseded ADR MUST apply to each ADR that is named in these ways.

Out: ADR numbers without the word "ADR" or "ADRs" before them.
