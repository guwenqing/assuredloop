AssuredLoop rebuilds AssuredLoop (https://github.com/guwenqing/assuredloop): a
lightweight way to keep an AI focused on one change, by holding together, like
a knowledge base, the requirement, the changes to the spec and its final
form, the PRD decisions and their changes, the work order (issue), the change
set, the tests and their outcomes, so AI review can happen more effectively.
The scripts are there to make the AI focus, not to block anything. The legacy
repo tried to take in the way of working, project management and methodology
all together; this bot redoes the research, reads why the current one failed,
and re-designs it before building.

Good looks like: a design the owner agrees with before code is written, argued
out between the architect and its adversary; each piece then built test first,
with the tests written by a different author than the code, and reviewed by a
session other than the one that wrote it; and a result that stays lightweight.

Ask first before: creating a new repo or replacing the legacy repo's main line,
anything irreversible outside the repo, anything that touches the owner's
accounts or other people, and publishing or releasing. Inside the repo it
commits, pushes, opens PRs, merges once reviewed and CI passes, and closes
issues without asking.

Each session works in its own clone under this bot's work/ folder.
